# 1. Vision

## The problem

South African mancos and LISPs run on **configuration-heavy administration platforms**. The brief names two: "Campus" and Silica (FNZ).

These platforms serve every client from **one generic engine**. Behaviour lives in thousands of parameters, rule tables and flags.

The cost lands on the manco:

- **Implementations take years.** The vendor configures, the manco tests, repeat.
- **Nobody can read the behaviour.** A fee or a cut-off is the result of ten settings interacting.
- **Change is risky.** A new class, a new product rule (two-pot), a new cut-off is another configuration project.
- **Controls are optional.** When everything is a setting, a financial control can be switched off by accident.
- **Values are stored, then trusted.** Balances and valuations are persisted and drift away from the facts.

## The thesis

> **Fixed kernel. Generated fit.**

1. A **small kernel** of financial building blocks: investment, redemption, switch, transfer, distribution, fees, pricing, cash, ledger, register.
2. The kernel's **controls and invariants are not configurable.** Not by the vendor. Not by the manco.
3. Everything that differs between mancos lives in a **Tenant Pack**: fund rules, workflows, policies, integrations, tests.
4. A pack is **generated by AI** from the manco's own documents and history, into a **constrained, readable, testable language**.
5. A pack **can only call kernel blocks.** It cannot bypass a control. It can make a control stricter, never looser.
6. A pack is **proven by simulation** against the manco's historical transactions before it goes live.

Configuration becomes a **per-manco codebase you can read**. Generated in days. With tests and provenance.

## Design principles

| # | Principle | Meaning |
|---|-----------|---------|
| P1 | **Kernel invariants are law** | No flag, role or pack can disable a control. |
| P2 | **Quantities are facts, values are views** | Store units, cash movements and published prices. Compute every value on read. |
| P3 | **Blocks talk through the ledger** | A block ends with a balanced journal and an event. The next block starts from a control account. |
| P4 | **Two time axes, always** | Every fact has an effective date and a knowledge date. Backdating is normal, explicit and owned. |
| P5 | **Every difference has an owner** | A price movement on a backdated or reversed deal is posted to a named account. Never absorbed silently. |
| P6 | **Generated, not configured** | Manco behaviour is a versioned, tested, provenance-annotated pack. |
| P7 | **Replayable** | Any balance, NAV or statement can be recomputed as at any point on either time axis. |
| P8 | **Controls first** | Maker-checker, segregation of duties and reconciliation are built into block boundaries. |
| P9 | **One fund or a thousand** | An account holds N instruments. The one-fund investor is N = 1. Who issues an instrument is an attribute, never a system boundary. |

## Scope

**In scope**

- One register for manco and LISP tenants. An account holds any number of instruments, own or external.
- Nominee (bulk) holdings across mancos, with bulk dealing and allocation.
- Cash management and settlement.
- Distributions, fees, regular instructions, corporate actions.
- Valuation on last known price, near real time.
- Financial controls, reconciliations, day close, audit.
- AI generation and lifecycle of Tenant Packs.
- Regulatory and tax outputs for South Africa.

**Interfaces, not v1 scope**

- **Fund accounting / NAV production.** The kernel defines the **pricing point contract**. A fund accounting module can be built later on the same principles.
- Portfolio and order management.
- CRM, document management, e-signature.

## Who it serves

- **Manco operations**: capture, approve, reconcile, close the day.
- **LISP operations**: bulk dealing, allocation, product rules, fee runs.
- **Portfolio managers**: expected net flows before the pricing point.
- **Finance**: control accounts, error account, cash forecast.
- **Trustee and auditors**: as-at views, backdating register, break register, provenance.
- **Advisers and investors**: values with an honest price basis.

## Success criteria

- A new manco goes live in **weeks**. A shadow run proves parity with its legacy system first.
- **Zero** unexplained breaks at day close.
- Every displayed value carries **its price and its date**.
- Every backdated or reversed cent has **a named owner and an approver**.
- A rule change ships as a **pack version with a diff, tests and sign-off**.
